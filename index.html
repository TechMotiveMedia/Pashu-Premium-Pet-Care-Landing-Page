Questions

Everything people ask first.

Do you accept animals without full vaccination records?

We accept any animal, vaccinated or not — that's exactly why the rescue desk exists. Boarding guests without current records are housed separately for a 10-day observation period, at no extra charge. We vaccinate in-house if you authorise it.

How does the live camera work?

Every suite has its own feed, accessible from the app or a private link after check-in. Audio is disabled by default for privacy. Footage is retained for 30 days in case you need it for a vet consultation.

What does a meet-and-greet involve?

A 20-minute visit, free, before any booking. Your pet meets the specific keeper who'd be assigned. If the chemistry isn't right — for you or for them — we pair differently or refund. About 1 in 8 first matches need a second pairing.

How long does the adoption process take?

Typically 3 to 7 days from first contact. It includes an application, a phone interview, a home visit and a signed adoption agreement. We don't home anyone to a household with children under six without a supervised introduction first.

Can I surrender my pet without judgement?

Yes, and we mean that literally. Roughly a third of our intakes are voluntary surrenders. There's no fee, no interrogation and no proof of "good reason". We'll take the animal the same day and tell you honestly where it ends up — including if it's adopted by someone else.

Are you licensed, and who is your vet?

Pashu Premium Pet Care is registered under the Pune Municipal Corporation's pet boarding classification, licence PMC/PB/2019/04417. Our supervising veterinarian is Dr. Anjali Menon, MVSc (Surgery), licence VC-4821, on call 24/7.
