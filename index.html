What we do

Three services. One promise —
your pet is never "just a client."

01 · Pet Food

Nutrition your pet actually asks for

Every recipe is designed with our in-house vet nutritionist and cooked in small batches — no fillers, no mystery meat. You tell us your pet's breed, age and allergies; we build the plan and deliver it fresh on a schedule that fits your kitchen, not a factory line.

  • Vet-formulated, breed & age specific recipes
  • Human-grade ingredients, cooked fresh weekly
  • Subscription or one-off boxes, pause anytime
Build a meal plan
02 · Pet Boarding

A second home — not a cage

Private suites, soft bedding and a play yard that sees more running than resting. Every guest gets a keeper assigned for the length of their stay, and you get a live feed so "how's he doing?" has an answer any hour of the night.

  • Private suites with 24/7 live camera access
  • 1 dedicated keeper per stay, not a rotating shift
  • On-call vet within 10 minutes, always
Reserve a suite
03 · Pet Walking

Every walk, an actual adventure

Trained, background-checked handlers who learn your pet's pace, favorite routes and quirks. Every walk is GPS-tracked with a photo drop, so it's never a guess whether the leash actually left the house.

  • GPS-tracked routes with live photo updates
  • Matched to a consistent, familiar handler
  • Solo or small-group pack walks available
