Our Services

We provide a comprehensive range of pet care services, tailored to the individual needs of pets and their families.

1. Dog Training & Behaviour Modification

We offer structured dog training programmes designed to improve communication, develop reliable manners, and address behavioural challenges.

Our services include board-and-train programmes, in-home training sessions, and personalised behaviour modification. Each programme is developed around the dog’s temperament, behavioural history, environment, and the family’s specific requirements.

Our goal is to build practical skills and establish clear, consistent communication between dogs and their handlers.

Enquire about dog training

2. Canine Nutrition Consultation

We provide personalised canine nutrition consultations and customised diet plans based on each dog’s individual dietary requirements.

Our approach considers feeding practices, ingredient selection, nutritional needs, and daily routines to support balanced and appropriate nutrition.

Every plan is developed around the dog’s specific needs rather than applying a one-size-fits-all approach.

Enquire about nutrition consultation

3. Pet Sitting

Professional pet care in the comfort of your pet’s own home.

Our pet-sitting services are designed for families who require dependable care while working, travelling, attending events, or managing other commitments.

Available options include drop-in visits, half-day sitting, full-day sitting, and overnight care. Each visit is organised around the pet’s feeding schedule, toilet breaks, exercise, companionship, and individual care instructions.

Enquire about pet sitting

4. Dog Walking

Our dog-walking service provides individual walks tailored to each dog’s age, fitness level, temperament, and exercise requirements.

We prioritise safe handling, leash manners, environmental awareness, and consistent routines. Every walk is treated as an opportunity for appropriate exercise, engagement, and positive enrichment.

Enquire about dog walking

5. Pet Transportation

We provide pet transportation for veterinary appointments, essential journeys, and other pet-related travel requirements.

Arrangements are discussed based on the pet’s needs, destination, handling requirements, and availability, with a strong emphasis on responsible handling and safe transportation.

Enquire about pet transportation

A home-style boarding option for pets while their families are away.

Our homestay service focuses on individual attention, established feeding routines, supervision, and maintaining familiar habits wherever practical. We discuss each pet’s requirements in advance to confirm suitability and establish appropriate care arrangements.

Advance enquiries are recommended to discuss availability and your pet’s specific needs.

We offer selected pet food and treats with an emphasis on ingredient quality and simpler formulations.

Where available, our product selection prioritises minimally processed options, products without unnecessary fillers, and preservative-free formulations where specifically verified.

We assist with adoption-related coordination to help animals find suitable, responsible homes.

Our approach emphasises informed decision-making and matching each animal’s needs with the prospective family’s lifestyle, environment, and long-term capacity for care.
